System diagram

Agent + RAG

Rendered from Mermaid in the spec. The repo keeps the Mermaid source.

Approach

Dispatchers ask in plain language ("who can take the Leeds drop at 3?"). The agent searches two years of route history for similar loads, checks live driver positions from telematics, and ranks drivers by predicted ETA and hours left on shift.

Why these models

smart handles tool calls and ranking explanations. fast summarises driver notes. ETA uses a small gradient-boosted model trained on their history; an LLM is not needed there.

Phases

PoC with mocked telematics and sample routes, then a 4-week pilot on one depot with the real feed.
